PRODUCT + ENGINEERING REVIEW / 1 NOVEMBER 2026

Earn closeness, not plot.

My first choice is A Secret. Pair it with a quiet, permanent count. Treat Bonus Scenes as a second-stage experiment, not the first implementation.

The story agent's scores are creative judgments, not technical readiness scores. The ideas are portable patterns, but their dialogue, imagery and emotional tone need authoring for each comic. Movement should never be necessary to understand the ending.

The three watches

  1. A Secret: a more intimate beat in today's location. Compatible with the existing activity trigger, daily cap and two-hour display. The new art is shared across activity kinds; walking does not require a walking pose. Draft lines test intimacy, not finished secret-writing. The strongest final version should reveal a tiny optional personal detail, not repeat exposition.
  2. Shared Count: the same companion moment plus a lasting rim tally. Each mark means one qualifying reward day, not one workout, total distance or all steps. This makes earning accumulate without a streak reset. It needs new persistence, caption substitution and rendering support. The tally is proposed; it is not in the shipped watch face.
  3. Bonus Scenes: a memory at five and ten qualifying days. Between milestones, the companion beat shows progress. Two new optional memories test collection value: Doc in the hangar and the Listener with a sleeping pod. Both are noncanonical writing proposals. A replay collection, milestone routing and persistence are new work, with hardware interaction, storage and resource-budget checks still required.

Current rules, not assumptions

  • Recorded running, walking or hiking qualifies at 3 km. Eligible other sports qualify at 15 minutes; not every sport is included.
  • There is also a step-based walking fallback: at least 2,000 steps at an average pace equivalent to 2,000 per 30 minutes since the sample. It is not a general daily step goal; the baseline resets after a long sampling gap.
  • At most one reward per local day. Detection happens when the watch face updates and checks activity history. It is not a guaranteed instant callback; earlier workouts can be missed when a later qualifying activity wins.
  • A reward displays for two hours, then advances the story one extra panel. The first wake after a new local day advances one panel. The ending has no action reward.
  • The caption is one line, capped at 16 characters, including the activity. Current dynamic tokens are distance and minutes only. The preview respects this cap; count and milestone substitutions would be new engine work.
  • The three previews use metric recorded activities with valid summary data. They do not simulate step sampling, history polling, missing fields, every excluded sport or imperial conversion. Production supports miles too; longer prefixes need their own caption variants.

These rules were checked against the local ActionState.mc, StoryState.mc and ComicView.mc. Garmin's UserActivity API exposes nullable start time, duration, distance and type. It does not supply workout weather or an all-time personal-best feed in that object.

The pacing decision

The preview defaults to a proposed daily-only story pace: the reward returns to the same story panel after two hours. This separates earning from consuming the plot. The selector also offers today's extra-step rule. With a reward every day, that rule reaches the ending after seven rewards, before the ten-day memory. Keeping current pacing means lowering milestones or defining a collection that carries across episodes.

No activity still completes all fourteen story panels. The simulated two-hour period is advanced manually, not a real wait. There is no sensor connection and the preview resets on reload.

A reusable comic frame

Keep the activity prefix and companion-beat visual language consistent. Do not mandate eye contact: Day 9's hands and fading pod are more truthful than a smiling portrait. Let each comic choose its voice, optional asides, milestone images and mood restrictions.

“Rarest wins” needs authored priorities, not guesswork. A future implementation should award each milestone once, preserve pending rewards, choose only among facts actually observed, and prevent a special caption from suppressing a promised unlock. There is no such arbitration engine in this preview.

Art direction and scope

Fifteen new generated images: thirteen scene-specific companion beats and two memories, through the existing OpenRouter image pipeline. Each companion image serves all three concepts so their writing and reward mechanics can be compared without an art-quality advantage. Bonus milestones have unique art.

The upper area is drawn scene-derived ink and halftone, not an empty reserved strip or a darkening overlay. Faces and important gestures are composed below the header. The clock, date, weather and heart rate are real HTML canvas overlays, not baked into the generated artwork.

The regular story is identical in all three watches. Panels 1, 7, 8 and 12 use the approved graphic studies; the other ten retain current masters and their existing production shading. This is a reward study, not a claim that all regular art has already been redesigned.

The circular preview uses an approximate font at a 390-unit reference size. A browser cannot certify Garmin display brightness, font metrics, palette conversion or legibility on your physical watch. Production still requires simulator and small-device checks. No Garmin source, story canon or store release was changed by this study.
